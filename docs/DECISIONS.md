# CANOPY design decisions

This log distinguishes adopted **concept assumptions** from future engineering decisions.

| ID | Concept decision | Validation owner / gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CAN-001 | Reference model uses 12 km², six districts and 60k residents | Urban design / alternative site comparison |
| CAN-002 | Test 15% building footprint, 48% habitat/water, 25% productive and 12% civic/mobility surfaces | Ecological assessment and GIS |
| CAN-003 | Passive-first, code-compliant courtyard blocks, generally 4–7 stories | Architecture, structure, accessibility and fire review |
| CAN-004 | Five resource loops with explicit import/export accounting | Engineering and public measurements |
| CAN-005 | Start with a 72-hour critical-load case, not two-week full-city autonomy | Electrical resilience model |
| CAN-006 | Local-first twin, ecological review gate and privacy by design | Security, scientists and public governance |
| CAN-007 | Keep novel structures and materials in isolated pilots pending approval | Independent certification |
| CAN-008 | Community land stewardship, affordability and voluntary participation | Housing and civic process |
| CAN-009 | Define CANOPY as Climate-Adaptive Network of Open Places for You | Project identity |

## Questions we must not conceal

- Which actual site has the legal, physical, ecological and financial capacity?
- How much native habitat exists before the project, and what is the measured baseline?
- How much energy arrives in winter, during multiday extreme events, and at each hour?
- What water rights, recharge rates, quality standards and emergency reserves apply?
- What food share can be grown without trading off habitat or major electricity use?
- What is the lifecycle cost per affordable home compared with a conventional reference?
- How will stakeholders choose, contest and reverse a technology-assisted recommendation?

The answer to unknown values is **not yet measured**, not an invented percentage.
