# CANOPY research and reality ledger

Every public claim should name a metric, boundary, evidence level, and falsification test.

## Buildable baseline, conditional on local conditions

- Riparian reserves, floodplain avoidance, restoration and habitat corridors
- Passive envelopes, appropriate orientation, shade, accessibility and ventilation
- Code-compliant mass timber or hybrid construction and material passports
- Solar, electrical storage and grid-connected microgrids after studies
- Verified wastewater treatment, fit-for-purpose reuse and monitored wetlands
- Walking, cycling, trams or BRT with emergency access
- Native landscape stewardship and public ecological indicators

## Pilot only, with explicit controls and rollback

- Kinetic facade panels versus simpler fixed shade
- Algae facade modules with measured net lifecycle benefit
- Mycelium/hemp/bamboo **non-structural** certified cassettes
- Aquifer thermal energy storage where hydrogeology and permissions allow
- Adaptive habitat management with human-reviewed outcomes
- Novel helical ducts/conduits against standard mechanical designs

## Not engineering commitments

- Self-healing structural mycelium or tectonic root foundations
- Over-unity energy production from resonance, wind geometry or artistic ratios
- Major power supply from footfall piezoelectric paving
- 70% caloric self-sufficiency from only the 12 km² site
- Fully closed material loops or no imported potable water
- Two weeks of full-city autonomy without load-specific hourly storage modeling
- Autonomous ecological enforcement, mass surveillance, or human displacement

## Experiment template

Every prototype should record:

- hypothesis and comparator/control;
- construction and operating cost;
- energy and material inputs;
- verified benefit and externality;
- reliability, maintenance and lifecycle;
- privacy and safety review;
- go/hold/stop thresholds;
- monitoring duration and open report.

## Literary inspiration versus physical law

Musical counterpoint, recursive patterns, electromagnetic phase systems and biomimetic shapes are useful **design prompts**. Proposed resonance indices, fractal coefficients and physical efficiencies must be defined and tested before they can become equations in a building specification. An analogy is not evidence.

## Provenance ledger

- **CANOPY original**: urban reference dimensions, courtyard architecture, six districts, integrated infrastructure.
- **SuperPhiVessel**: symphonic geometry, polyphase and evolutionary design hypotheses; separated from empirical claims.
- **Aurelia (Grok)**: passive-first design, resource sharing, local autonomy.
- **Symbio (Kimi)**: the five circulatory systems, site selection and material inventories.
- **Aluvia (Claude)**: watershed-first urbanism, public safeguards, privacy, ecological review.
- **Oikos-1 (Gemini)**: adaptable envelopes and biomaterial experiments, only if validated.

These names represent ideation contributions, not verification of any engineering result.
